## Problem Statement

The 2026-10-16 design tested whether Danish non-developer organisations would pay 349 kr./md. for a hosted aarshjul editor. It rested on the premise "the only tool for them today is Plandisc at EUR 27 to 39 per user per month with no free plan." That premise is false. Two Danish products, Circlio (circlio.dk) and Yearspan (yearspan.dk, company registered 2025-12-16), ship hosted aarshjul editors with free tiers, sharing links, export and integrations, aimed at foreninger, HR and kommuner. A priced door for an unbuilt editor now measures a premium price against free shipped products, so a low click rate cannot be read.

The question this design answers: will organisations pay to get the plan they already have turned into a finished wheel, a job none of the free editors do for them?

## Target User and Narrowest Wedge

Target user: the secretary of a municipal or regional MED committee (HovedMED, FagMED, LokalMED) or the HR secretariat behind it. They own a plan that already exists as text, need it shown to the committee and staff. Hypothesis, tested by the call script's who-owns and who-orders questions: they can spend 1,500 kr. without procurement.

Wedge: "Vi laver jeres egen plan om til et faerdigt aarshjul." 1,500 kr. one-off.

Free sample versus paid wheel (the difference goes into every email and call):
- Sample, free, sent unasked: one screen-resolution PNG of the plan exactly as published, one ring set, uncorrected, with a diagonal "PROEVE" watermark across the wheel (CEO review D5.1), so the sample shows the result without replacing the paid file.
- Paid wheel, 1,500 kr.: drawn from the committee's current plan as they send it (not the published copy), every ring they want (for example FagMED and LokalMED), print-ready A3 PDF plus PNG and SVG, no sample line, one round of corrections, delivered within five working days of the written order.

Foreninger are second: smallest budgets, DGI closed that channel, and both free editors target them.

## Constraints

- No new code for this test. The MIT engine in src/ draws every wheel.
- Founder time is limited. Budget, itemised: lead research for 8 new plans and named recipients 2 h; 8 sample wheels at about 20 min 3 h; emails 1 h; calls to 11 leads at up to 3 attempts of about 10 min 5.5 h; the federation chairman call 0.5 h; step 0 page and checker change 1.5 h; CVR and e-invoice setup 1 h; delivering up to 2 paid wheels with corrections 3 h. Conditional: if exactly 1 order arrives, the extension (5 more leads: research, samples, emails, calls) adds about 6 h and two weeks, to 2026-12-11; the founder commits to that extension before the first call, so a single order is never left unread. If the founder cannot give the base plus the conditional time, the test is not run rather than run half.
- Hard gate (founder decision D8, 2026-11-02): nothing in this plan that sells runs until the founder holds the employer's written consent. That covers the calls about the 2026-10-19 offers, new offers, the CVR registration, any order, and pushing T1 to gh-pages. The consent request (drafted 2026-11-02) states: pre-existing MIT library since 2025-10-18, own time only, no Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Teams or intranet work, no employer customers, no use of work knowledge, and that Circalify stays the founder's. - Accepted legal risk (CEO review D3, founder's choice): the sample emails in step 2 are unsolicited electronic marketing under markedsfoeringsloven par. 10, which also covers public authorities; a complaint can bring a fine of at least 10,000 kr. or 100 kr. per mail. ## Premises

Agreed by the founder on 2026-10-30.

1. The 2026-10-16 gate is retired. Free Danish editors exist, so the 349 kr. door cannot produce a readable result. The page stays up and is no longer the test.
2. The product is the finished wheel made from the customer's existing plan, not an editor.
3. First buyer: municipal or regional MED committees and HR secretariats. Foreninger second.
4. Each offer carries a free sample built from the customer's own public plan. The paid wheel is only made after a written order (email or purchase order); the sequence is written order, delivery within five working days, then an electronic invoice on the buyer's normal payment terms. A free pilot is not a result.
5. No Pro software is built until at least 3 paid wheels show a repeat need, the yearly update being the expected repeat.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Ten spec wheels plus phone follow-up (chosen)
Follow up the three 2026-10-19 offers by phone, then send eight more to MED committees whose aarshjul is public as text. Effort S (human: about 2 days over four weeks / CC: about 3 h for the samples). Fastest paid verdict, no new code, the calls are the missing conversations.

### Approach B: Plan-to-wheel intake tool plus yearly update price
Rejected for now: builds software before anyone has paid, the failure the old gate existed to prevent; two prices in one offer blur the answer. Revisit after premise 5 is met.

### Approach C: Whole-municipality MED package at 10,000 to 15,000 kr.
Rejected for now: longer decisions, possible procurement rules, fewer attempts. It is the upsell if a single committee pays.

Ruled out earlier in the session: keeping the 349 kr. door as the test (unreadable against free editors), selling the library to developers (no paying signal from developers), stopping Pro entirely (abandons the one real behavioural signal).
